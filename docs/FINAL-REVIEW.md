A100k-Root90-day season also requires1080 SOL in single-call signature fees versus20 SOL resource-fee income. New ADVANCE freezes denominators independently of each Position's exact lazy accrual; END no longer waits for debt. Old budgets/beneficiaries stay protected and residue waits for completeness. Event volume, claimant history, rent and independent security/release review remain gates; the repair does not promise unlimited throughput or free keeper work.
- **Research privacy is not supplied by Artifact sealing.** Artifacts are public bytes. A player who uploads a new graph before COMMIT can expose it to another independently funded player. The implemented typed delegated-synthesis workflow plans private local experiment →COMMIT binding H7/future manufacture nonce/salt →public upload/verification →actual manufacture/reveal within the window, reading actual phase/clock/account state. The contract judges its explicit commitments/Disclosure/Knowledge facts; it cannot determine an off-chain inventor. No new on-chain research-ownership or fee mechanism is introduced.
- **Current novelty headroom remains unproved after a genuine discovery.** Legal C310 was found under unchanged mathematics and completed475 actual signed transactions on old76b/current16/frontier316 through Candidate, Winner, K, two claims and exit. - Generic SDK dynamic accounts require actual decoded state and a phase-aware planner. Native handlers remain the final authority. Current fixed tables/typed payloads do not turn an arbitrary client-supplied account list or a successful serialization into biological/ownership evidence. ## Explicit multiple-storage-error limitation

A kind2 corrected Knowledge row is anchored to its sealed verified Safety proof, rather than repeatedly invalidated because its original certificate is still wrong. If an original Root had only a link error, was corrected, and its supposedly immutable certificate later suffers a separate program storage error, that later Root error is not independently faultable through the already-invalidated original Knowledge row. No public instruction permits either arbitrary certificate write. Covering multiple arbitrary program-storage corruptions would require a separately reviewed proof design; no admin setter or retroactive debt rewriting was added. Independent security review must specifically assess this limitation.
